- Honey finder: a name is never proof. A seller is listed only when a directory's product field or tag says honey (OpenStreetMap `shop=honey`/`craft=beekeeper`/`produce`, a USDA product list or description, a Nostr listing tagged honey or naming bee products) or when its own homepage names at least two bee products — beeswax, apiary, raw or comb honey, honeybees and the like. "Honey-Do" handymen and "sweet as honey" shops no longer qualify. Each seller now says who confirmed it (`confirmed_by`).
